Put your mp3 files here:

  background.mp3   -> looping background music (Section 4)
  song-1.mp3        -> playlist song 1 (Section 11)
  song-2.mp3        -> playlist song 2
  song-3.mp3        -> playlist song 3
  song-4.mp3        -> playlist song 4

The site checks for these gracefully - if a file is missing, nothing
breaks, the player just won't produce sound yet.
